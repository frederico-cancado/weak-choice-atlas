# Related articles

Primary sources for algebraic closures without choice, finite compactness principles, and symmetric models. Links and the licenses of the two mirrored papers were checked on **14 October 2026**. The notes distinguish established results in the literature from applications developed in the accompanying article; they do not certify historical novelty.

## Algebraic closures and weak choice

The following sources are provided as external links; their files are not redistributed in this bundle.

- **Bernhard Banaschewski (1992).** *Algebraic closure without choice.* Mathematical Logic Quarterly **38**, 383–385. Proves the classical implication from the Boolean Ultrafilter Theorem to both existence and uniqueness of algebraic closures.
  [DOI and publisher](https://doi.org/10.1002/malq.19920380136) · [Institution-hosted scan](https://metaphor.ethz.ch/x/2025/fs/401-2004-00L/sc/Banaschewski.pdf)

- **Hans Läuchli (1962/63).** *Auswahlaxiom in der Algebra.* Commentarii Mathematici Helvetici **37**, 1–18. Classical permutation-model constructions underlying failures of algebraic choice statements.
  [Primary archive](https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/id/PPN358147735_0037?tify=%7B%22view%22%3A%22info%22%2C%22pages%22%3A%5B5%5D%7D)

- **Wilfrid Hodges (1976).** *Läuchli's algebraic closure of Q.* Mathematical Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society **79**(2), 289–297. Discusses the transferred Läuchli closure and failure of uniqueness over the rationals in pure ZF. This does not by itself assert that every field in the same model has a closure.
  [DOI and publisher](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100052282)

- **Amitayu Banerjee and Alexa Gopaulsingh (2023).** *On the Erdős–Dushnik–Miller theorem without AC.* Bulletin of the Polish Academy of Sciences. Mathematics **71**, 1–21. Section 1.1 records the ZFA qualification in the classical uniqueness nonimplications.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4064/ba221221-6-6) · [Publisher and open full text](https://www.impan.pl/en/publishing-house/journals-and-series/bulletin-polish-acad-sci-math/all/71/1/115201/on-the-erdos-dushnik-miller-theorem-without-ac)

- **Paul Howard and Jean E. Rubin (1998).** *Consequences of the Axiom of Choice.* Mathematical Surveys and Monographs **59**, American Mathematical Society. Forms 14, 69, and 233 catalog BPI, algebraic-closure existence, and conditional uniqueness; Note 41 records Pincus's permutation-model result. Historical matrix entries should not be read as a certification of current openness.
  [DOI and publisher](https://doi.org/10.1090/surv/059)

## Symmetric models and transfer

- **David Pincus (1972).** *Zermelo–Fraenkel consistency results by Fraenkel–Mostowski methods.* Journal of Symbolic Logic **37**, 721–743. Transfer methods from permutation models. Preserving a universal statement about all fields requires verification of the applicable transfer hypotheses.
  [DOI and publisher](https://doi.org/10.2307/2272420)

- **Asaf Karagila (2021 revision).** *Iterated failures of choice.* arXiv:1911.09285v3, 23 July 2021. Sections 5.2 and 6.1 discuss symmetric copies and multiple algebraic closures. The result retains hypotheses about the existence and automorphisms of a supplied closure; it should not be cited as a universal existence theorem.
  [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.09285v3) · [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.09285v3)

- **Asaf Karagila (2019).** *Preserving Dependent Choice.* Bulletin of the Polish Academy of Sciences. Mathematics **67**, 19–29. General preservation theorems for dependent choice in symmetric extensions.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4064/ba8169-12-2018) · [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.11301v3) · [Accepted manuscript](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/69607/1/Accepted_Manuscript.pdf)

- **Norbert Brunner and Jean E. Rubin (1986).** *Permutation models and topological groups.* Rendiconti del Seminario Matematico della Università di Padova **76**, 149–161. Background on permutation-model methods and their group-theoretic structure.
  [Primary archive](https://www.numdam.org/item/RSMUP_1986__76__149_0/)

- **Asaf Karagila and Jonathan Schilhan (2026).** *Intermediate models and Kinna–Wagner Principles.* Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society **154**(1), 393–403. Small violations of choice and Kinna–Wagner principles in symmetric-model settings.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/proc/17425) · [Institutional accepted manuscript](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/229337/)

- **Asaf Karagila and Jonathan Schilhan (2026 preprint).** *Towards a theory of symmetric extensions.* arXiv:2602.17338, first submitted 19 February 2026. A general reference for the structure and iteration of symmetric extensions.
  [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17338)

## Boolean-valued algebra, compactness, and further consequences

- **Kay Smith (1984).** *Commutative regular rings and Boolean-valued fields.* Journal of Symbolic Logic **49**(1), 281–297. Establishes a relationship between commutative regular rings and Boolean-valued fields, including algebraic-closure constructions in that setting. This is background for the formalism, rather than an attribution for a specific equivariant lifting lemma.
  [DOI and publisher](https://doi.org/10.2307/2274110)

- **Erhard Aichinger, Peter Mayr, and Ralph McKenzie (2014).** *On the number of finite algebraic structures.* Journal of the European Mathematical Society **16**(8), 1673–1686. Finite relatedness for finite algebras with a Malcev term, relevant to presenting finite group constraints by finite relational templates.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/JEMS/472) · [Publisher record](https://ems.press/journals/jems/articles/11811)

- **Henri Lombardi and M.-F. Roy (1991).** *Théorie constructive élémentaire des corps ordonnés.* Publications mathématiques de la Faculté des sciences de Besançon. Théorie des nombres, article 3, 1–23. Constructive ordered-field theory through the construction of real closures.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5802/pmb.a-64) · [Primary archive](https://numdam.org/articles/10.5802/pmb.a-64/) · [Full text](https://www.numdam.org/article/PMB_1991____A3_0.pdf)

- **Horst Herrlich and Eleftherios Tachtsis (2017).** *On the solvability of systems of linear equations over the ring Z of integers.* Commentationes Mathematicae Universitatis Carolinae **58**, 241–260. Weak-choice strength of solvability principles for linear systems.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.14712/1213-7243.2015.207) · [Journal PDF](https://cmuc.karlin.mff.cuni.cz/pdf/cmuc1702/tachtsel.pdf)

- **Stefan Geschke, Robert Lubarsky, and Mona Rahn (2015 preprint).** *Choice and the Hat Game.* Dated 13 March 2015. Parity functions and the obstruction provided by the Baire property.
  [Author-hosted full text](https://www.math.uni-hamburg.de/home/geschke/papers/Choice_and_the_hat_game6.pdf)

- **Luke Serafin (2023).** *Ultrafilters, Transversals, and the Hat Game.* arXiv:2311.07886v2, 2 December 2023. Separates hat-game parity principles from other choice consequences. A parity function in this sense need not be additive.
  [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07886v2)

## Scope of the bibliography

The relevant classical failures of existence and uniqueness, their transfer arguments, and the finite-template compactness results are separate assertions. They do not automatically establish a conjunction such as universal existence together with nonuniqueness in one model. The accompanying article supplies its own arguments for its stated conclusions. The reverse implication from conditional uniqueness to universal existence is described there as unresolved **within that work**, without asserting a complete determination of its status throughout the literature.
